📊 Exhibit sums (rough totals)

Like reading a row of a case exhibit. Three tricks: ① start with the biggest cell, ② pair up the small cells, ③ keep one running total in hundreds (11.5 → 16 → 20…), then type your rough total. Within ±10% counts as right — speed matters more than precision. Answer in the same unit as the numbers (e.g. 2200 or 2.2k).

📐 Percentages

The two percentage moves every case needs: "What % is it?" (2,170 out of 5,000 → 43%) and "How much is X%?" (55% of 480 → 264). Close is good enough: within ±2 points or ±3% counts as right. Type 43 or 43%.
